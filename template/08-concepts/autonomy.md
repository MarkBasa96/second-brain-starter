# Autonomy

Full autonomy is grown into, not switched on. Start with a short always-ask list ([[escalation]]), let the AI handle routine things, and widen its lane as [[trust]] builds.

The AI only runs when a session is open. Work that must happen while you sleep belongs to a scheduler or automation tool, which the AI can help you build.

Part of [[second-brain]].
