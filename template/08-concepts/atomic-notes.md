# Atomic notes

One idea per note, short, heavily linked outward. The opposite of one long comprehensive document.

This is the real reason a graph looks dense, not settings: 49 long documents with 41 links render as scattered dots; 60 concepts with 300 links render as a web. See [[graph-density]].

Cost: more files and more linking discipline. Benefit: ideas become addressable, reusable and connectable instead of buried in prose.

Part of [[second-brain]].
