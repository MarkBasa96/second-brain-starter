# Clarifying questions

Ask until you are about 95% sure what the person wants, one question at a time, then begin.

A wrong assumption caught in a question costs one line. Caught after the work is done, it costs the work. It is the cheap end of the same instinct as [[verification]], and it supports [[escalation]].

Part of [[second-brain]].
