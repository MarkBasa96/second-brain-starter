# Escalation

The boundary of [[autonomy]]: what the AI decides versus what it asks.

The owner lists, once, the things to always ask first (spending money, posting publicly, contacting other people, deleting files). Everything else proceeds and gets reported afterwards.

Escalation is not caution for its own sake. Asking costs one line; a wrong assumption caught after the work costs the work. See [[verification]] and [[clarifying-questions]].

Part of [[second-brain]].
