# Welcome

Your first journal entry. Journals are one note per day or session: what you built,
decided and learned. The wrap-up skill writes them for you at the end of a session.

New to this? Read [[second-brain]], then [[00-concept-map]]. Press Ctrl+G (Cmd+G on
Mac) to see your brain as a graph. It starts small. Every note you write and link
adds a star.
